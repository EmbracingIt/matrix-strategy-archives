The initial collection is deliberately designed so EVERY Market Phase has at least three genuinely suitable strategies.

Use the following as the canonical assignments.

-------------------------
EARLY RECOVERY
-------------------------

1. Accumulation LP
Phase Fit: 95

Reason:
The market is improving but directional conviction remains uncertain. Accumulation LP allows the investor to continue acquiring the target asset through weakness/ranging while earning fees rather than making one large directional entry.

2. Stablecoin DCA Into Crypto
Phase Fit: 90

Reason:
Early recovery is an ideal environment for staged deployment. Conditions have improved enough to justify increasing exposure, but uncertainty remains high enough that gradual purchases are preferable to committing all stablecoin capital immediately.

3. Yield-Funded Accumulation
Phase Fit: 85

Reason:
Most capital remains productive while generated yield is gradually redirected into crypto exposure. This gives participation in recovery while preserving a conservative capital base.

-------------------------
BTC-LED EXPANSION
-------------------------

1. Blue-Chip Asset Lending
Phase Fit: 90

Reason:
BTC and other blue-chip assets can remain held while generating incremental lending yield. The strategy avoids prematurely rotating out of the asset currently leading the market.

2. Borrow Against BTC
Phase Fit: 95

Reason:
Allows BTC holders to access stablecoin liquidity without selling the asset that is currently leading the expansion. The primary risk is liquidation if BTC reverses sharply, so conservative LTV management is critical.

3. Bull-Market Hold + Yield
Phase Fit: 85

Reason:
During BTC leadership the priority should remain preserving directional upside. Yield components should be additive and should not materially cap participation in the trend.

-------------------------
ALT EXPANSION
-------------------------

1. ETH Liquid Staking
Phase Fit: 90

Reason:
As capital rotates from BTC toward ETH, liquid staking preserves directional ETH exposure while generating native staking return and retaining DeFi liquidity.

2. SOL Liquid Staking
Phase Fit: 95

Reason:
SOL often behaves as a higher-beta asset during broad risk-on expansion. Liquid staking combines directional participation with staking rewards while maintaining composability.

3. Bull-Market Hold + Yield
Phase Fit: 90

Reason:
During broad market expansion the largest opportunity cost is capping upside. The strategy focuses on maintaining core directional positions while generating incremental yield where possible.

-------------------------
LATE BULL / DISTRIBUTION
-------------------------

1. Distribution LP
Phase Fit: 95

Reason:
Liquidity positioned above market can systematically convert appreciated crypto into stablecoins while earning fees, creating a rules-based method for distributing exposure into strength.

2. Covered Call Yield
Phase Fit: 90

Reason:
Selling upside optionality becomes more attractive when expected incremental upside is deteriorating and volatility/premium remains elevated. The trade-off of capped upside becomes less costly late in the cycle.

3. Stablecoin Yield Rotation
Phase Fit: 80

Reason:
Capital already taken off directional exposure can remain productive by rotating across conservative stablecoin venues rather than sitting completely idle.

-------------------------
CAPITULATION / DELEVERAGING
-------------------------

1. Deleveraging Into Weakness
Phase Fit: 100

Reason:
The strategy is directly designed for this environment. Falling collateral values and forced liquidations mean the priority is reducing leverage before liquidation thresholds are reached.

2. Stablecoin Reserve Strategy
Phase Fit: 95

Reason:
Capital preservation, liquidity and optionality become more valuable than maximizing return during violent deleveraging and protocol stress.

3. Capital-Preservation DeFi Portfolio
Phase Fit: 90

Reason:
Diversification across conservative lending, liquid stable reserves and lower-risk venues reduces dependence on one directional asset or one protocol during systemic stress.

-------------------------
LOW-VOL COMPRESSION
-------------------------

1. Range-Bound Yield LP
Phase Fit: 95

Reason:
A stable trading range allows concentrated liquidity to remain active and earn fees without repeatedly moving out of range.

2. Concentrated Liquidity Provision
Phase Fit: 90

Reason:
Lower realized volatility makes tighter ranges more practical, increasing capital efficiency and fee generation per unit of deposited capital.

3. Delta-Neutral LP
Phase Fit: 85

Reason:
The strategy can monetize trading activity while reducing directional exposure, making it attractive when the market lacks a persistent trend.

-------------------------
HIGH-VOLATILITY CHOP
-------------------------

1. Delta-Neutral LP
Phase Fit: 90

Reason:
Large two-way moves create fee opportunities, while the hedge reduces the directional exposure that would otherwise dominate an LP position.

2. Hedged Concentrated Liquidity
Phase Fit: 95

Reason:
Concentrated liquidity remains potentially lucrative during active markets, but frequent reversals cause rapidly changing delta. Active hedging is therefore essential.

3. Market-Neutral Yield Stack
Phase Fit: 90

Reason:
Repeated directional reversals make outright directional exposure unreliable. A market-neutral stack attempts to isolate yield while offsetting most price exposure.

STRATEGY_001 — ACCUMULATION LP

Preserve the strong existing documentation if it already exists.

Core idea:

Use concentrated liquidity at/under spot so stablecoins progressively convert into the target asset during weakness while earning swap fees.

The intentional downward conversion is part of the strategy, not an accidental side effect.

STRATEGY_002 — STABLECOIN DCA INTO CRYPTO

This should NOT simply be:

“Buy $100 of ETH every week.”

Make it a DeFi capital-allocation strategy.

Stablecoin capital remains liquid/productive and is systematically deployed into chosen crypto assets according to predetermined sizing/time/risk rules.

Explain the advantages and disadvantages relative to lump-sum deployment.

STRATEGY_003 — YIELD-FUNDED ACCUMULATION

The principal stays primarily inside a yield-producing strategy.

Generated yield is periodically redirected into BTC/ETH/SOL or another target asset.

Important distinction:

The user is primarily accumulating using RETURN GENERATED BY CAPITAL, rather than continuously consuming the principal.

==================================================

STRATEGY_004 — BLUE-CHIP ASSET LENDING

The user already wants to hold BTC/ETH-like exposure.

Instead of leaving the asset idle, it is supplied to lending markets.

Explain:

- incremental return
- utilization/rate variability
- smart-contract risk
- borrower-side mechanics
- why the lender itself generally does not face liquidation merely for lending an unborrowed asset

Do not confuse lending with leveraged borrowing.

==================================================

STRATEGY_005 — BORROW AGAINST BTC

BTC remains collateral.

Stablecoins or another asset are borrowed against it.

The user avoids selling BTC but introduces:

- debt
- interest cost
- liquidation risk

This is NOT free liquidity.

The documentation must strongly emphasize conservative LTV management.

Maintain core directional exposure.

Explain why this may outperform aggressive LP/distribution strategies during strong expansion.

STRATEGY_010 — COVERED CALL YIELD

Hold the underlying asset while selling calls against part/all of the position.

Return:

option premium.

Trade-off:

upside above strike is surrendered.

Explain why this becomes particularly interesting late in a bull cycle or in a sideways market.

Do not falsely describe a covered call as full downside protection.

Premium only provides a limited downside buffer.

==================================================

STRATEGY_011 — STABLECOIN YIELD ROTATION

Rotate stablecoins between conservative yield venues when net yield/risk becomes meaningfully more attractive elsewhere.

Do NOT recommend constantly chasing the highest headline APY.

Explain evaluation of:

- protocol risk
- stablecoin risk
- liquidity
- incentives
- transaction/bridge costs
- yield durability

==================================================

This is a RISK-MANAGEMENT strategy.

Not an alpha strategy.

The objective is systematically reducing leverage as market structure deteriorates.

Possible actions:

- repay debt
- add collateral
- reduce directional exposure
- close leveraged loops

The methodology should prioritize survival over maximizing return.

STRATEGY_018 — HEDGED CONCENTRATED LIQUIDITY

This is more active/sophisticated than generic Delta-Neutral LP.

A concentrated LP has rapidly changing delta as price moves through its range.

The hedge must therefore be actively adjusted.

High-Volatility Chop is particularly relevant because:

- trading volume may generate fees
- frequent reversals generate changing directional exposure
- active hedging attempts to control that exposure

Clearly describe execution complexity.

funding

imperfect hedge
